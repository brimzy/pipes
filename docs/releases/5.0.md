### Player depth and motion refinement

- Exact Oxocarbon neutral colours from the reference theme.
- Shared surface elevations and matching artwork/shadow contours.
- A recessed play/pause control with spring feedback and an animated symbol.
- Search expands before taking focus; pages and navigation transition smoothly.
- Artwork and queue swipes track the finger directly and spring back on release.

Pipes 5.0 brings artwork-led discovery, a floating circular player, gesture queue controls, and a refined Oxocarbon design with Libron typography.

- Charcoal surfaces with purposeful blue, cyan, and violet accents.
- Libron regular and bold throughout the app, bundled locally with the font license and copyright notices.
- Recognizable navigation icons; decorative dot-and-dash strips removed.
- Swipe right to add a song to the queue, left to remove an upcoming entry, and hold for song actions. Screen-reader actions remain available.
- Drag the circular mini-player inside the app; release to spring toward the nearest side. Tap to open the player and hold for playback controls.
- Artwork-focused Home, Library, artist pages, and player, without vinyl simulations. Home refreshes around song selection.
- Immediate seek-time preview with a growing cyan glow and relevance-preserving radio recommendations.

### Upgrade from 4.x

Download `app-release.apk` from this page and install it over your existing official Pipes app to keep your library and settings. The 4.x updater does not recognize 5.x tags, so this first upgrade requires a direct download. Pipes 5.0 supports subsequent 5.x updates in the app.

Android 8.0 or newer is required. This release is built, tested, linted, and signed by GitHub Actions. Visual and touch verification on a running device remains pending.
