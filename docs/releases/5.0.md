### Spacing, artwork and Explore

- Consistent 24dp page margins, 16dp artwork columns, 8dp title spacing and 4dp metadata spacing across Home and Explore.
- Muted, two-physical-pixel artwork edges across Home, search, artist/release pages and the player. Softer shadows and matching shapes replace heavy default outlines.
- Outline-free floating navigation dock and Library action buttons; functional keyboard-focus outlines remain visible.
- Explore now browses moods and genres with playable song previews, a direction based on the current artist, refresh, retry and more search results. Recent selections are cached during the session.
- Removed the duplicate two-song showcase from the top of Library. Saved songs remain in the collection.

Pipes 5.0 uses a true black canvas, Oxocarbon charcoal surfaces, neutral Libron typography and restrained cyan playback controls.

- Artwork-led Home refreshes around song selection; song radio preserves source relevance.
- Swipe right to queue, left to remove an upcoming entry, and hold for song actions. Screen readers expose the same actions.
- Drag the circular mini-player inside the app, tap to open, or hold for playback controls.
- Seek-time previews track the finger immediately with a growing glow; the player uses original artwork.
- Android Auto browses saved likes, playlists and completed downloads, with saved-library search.
- The separate iPhone release includes CarPlay for imported local audio. Vehicle use requires Apple-approved CarPlay signing.

### Upgrade from 4.x

Download `app-release.apk` and install it over your existing official Pipes app to keep your library and settings. The 4.x updater does not recognize 5.x tags, so this first upgrade requires a direct download. Pipes 5.0 supports subsequent 5.x updates in the app.

Android 8.0 or newer is required. GitHub Actions builds, tests, lints and signs this release. Visual and touch verification on a running device and car/head-unit testing remain pending.
