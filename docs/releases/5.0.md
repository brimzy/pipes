### A more refined player
- Added **Play next** to song actions and screen reader actions across artwork, search, library, and queue rows. It inserts after the current song, moves an already queued song forward, and honors the request with shuffle enabled.
- Replaced gray panels with deep ink surfaces, soft shadows, and subtle edge lighting. The full player, minimized player, and navigation dock now share the same finish.
- Added a persistent artwork backdrop that stays visible while scrolling and around the navigation dock.
- Simplified the main play button to a single illuminated control and gave the album artwork more depth.
- Made the minimized player more compact, with a slim seek bar, elapsed/total time, and play/pause with a loading indicator. Tap the artwork or title to reopen the full player.
- Added a close button to both player states. Closing stops playback, clears the queue, and cancels pending playback requests. The down arrow still minimizes without interrupting the song.
- Kept the full-player waveform and transport controls visible on shorter screens, along with song radio, queue access, likes, and saving to Commute Mix.

Install `app-release.apk` over your existing official Pipes app to keep your library and settings. Android 8.0 or newer is required.
