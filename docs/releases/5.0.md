### Cleaner search
- Added a charcoal current-song player above navigation with play/pause, the cyan waveform seek slider, and elapsed/total time. Tap the artwork or title to reopen the full player.
- Kept the full-player seek slider and transport controls below a flexible artwork area so they remain visible on shorter screens.

- Shortened the search placeholder to “Search music” and removed the separate link button and dialog. Paste YouTube or Spotify links directly into search.
- Ranked music and video results together by query-word matches in the song title and artist, so searches such as “Tokyo weeknd” surface matching recordings ahead of partial matches.
- Pasted YouTube radio links still show the seed song followed by related music.
- Preserved the restored full-player waveform design.

Install `app-release.apk` over your existing official Pipes app to keep your library and settings. Android 8.0 or newer is required.
