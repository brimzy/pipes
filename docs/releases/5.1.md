# Pipes 5.1

A monochrome refresh with charcoal surfaces, neutral waveform seeking and a silver play control. Color appears in selected navigation, shuffle, repeat and liked states.

The full player opens into the artwork atmosphere without a boxed transport panel. Larger responsive artwork, a shared spacing grid, subtle drifting stars and tactile playback actions bring the controls together. Home uses a smaller header and a wider featured cover to show more music.

Discovery refreshes when saved artist preferences change and prioritizes those artists over generic mood searches. Suggestions no longer recursively seed personalized artist browsing. With no preferences, a varied mood feed helps you begin; playing music and liking artists tune subsequent recommendations.

The waveform retains drag previews and release-to-seek behavior, with a light haptic on commit. Playback, queue, radio, playlists and local audio remain available.

## Live music & swipe controls

- Swipe the mini-player song row left for next or right for previous, with spring feedback and a light haptic. The seek bar keeps its own drag gesture.
- Browse a swipeable artwork carousel on Home. Your current track leads with live audio motion; tap to open the full player or play a discovery card.
- The main waveform now responds to actual decoded audio amplitude. Pause, buffering and scrubbing restore the original seek pattern. No microphone permission or audio recording is needed.
- Audio analysis passes playback samples through unchanged; visual updates stop while the app is in the background.
